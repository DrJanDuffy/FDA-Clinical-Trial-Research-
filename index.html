Where this data comes from

Every result is fetched live, in your browser, straight from the official source. Nothing is cached, re-hosted or edited.

  • ClinicalTrials.gov — U.S. NIH registry of clinical studies (API v2)
  • Europe PMC — indexes PubMed/MEDLINE, PMC and preprint servers
  • openFDA — Drugs@FDA, device PMA & 510(k), enforcement reports

Use of this data is subject to each provider's terms — ClinicalTrials.gov, Europe PMC and openFDA.

Important

TrialScope is a search tool, not medical advice. Results are provided as-is from public databases and may be incomplete or out of date — registry records are updated by sponsors, not in real time. Never start, stop or change treatment based on what you find here. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional, and confirm anything important against the original record before acting on it.
